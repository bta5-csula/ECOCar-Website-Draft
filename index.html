High-Voltage Battery

Cells become modules, modules become a pack, and voltage, current, energy and thermal limits shape the entire vehicle architecture. Designed, modeled, and assembled by Cal State LA students.

P4 Rear Drive Integration

The electric rear axle, motors and inverters fit beneath the 2026 Jeep Cherokee with sound mounts, load paths and suspension clearance, converting the platform into an electrified all-wheel-drive machine.

Embedded Controls & CAN

Vehicle modules communicate over a coordinated bus. The team decodes real-time signals, programs vehicle supervisory logic, and designs fail-safe state machines across every subsystem.

Thermal & Safety Systems

Cooling plates, coolant loops, contactors and high-voltage interlocks keep components within safe operating envelopes through intense dyno cycles and real-world proving runs.
